Starships comprise a plethora of vehicles capable of space travel, ranging from unmanned delivery drones all the way to mobile cities. Owning a starship is often a mercantile affair, akin to owning a business. To own a starship is to take liability for its passengers, and to brave the dangers of deep space. The galaxy is a vast place, filled with asteroids, comets, and pirates. Space travel often takes you far beyond the reach of civilization, meaning a ship malfunction can leave you stranded for weeks at a time, and an attack can leave you fighting for your life.

It is rare for an average citizen to own a starship, but common for them to voyage on them. Starship fare is usually around 50 credits per day per passenger, and includes meals and amenities. As such, more luxurious starships cost more to travel on. Hitch hiking is common, and most spaceports have rentable starships (for a very high price), or a place for would-be passengers to seek lodgings on ships coming and going.

Starships consume 1 fuel each day of travel, and 1 food per passenger per day. A starship can hold fuel equal to 5 times its max crew, and food equal to 50 + 100 per empty expansion bay.

# Starship Stats

Starships have a level (average level of crew), an AC (10+Base+Speed+Other), an SC (10+level+other), a shield, a speed, and of course health. 

Stats unique to starships are Size Class, Max Crew, Expansion Bays, Weapon Ports, and Mod Slots
Size Class is a ship's categorical size. Starships of Large and Huge size take up 3 hexes, and Gargantuan and Flagship size take up 6 or 7 hexes.

Starships of any size can be operated by a singular person in most situations, but there is a **Maximum Crew Limit**. This does not represent the maximum number of people aboard a ship, but is the number of passengers who can meaningfully take actions in a single turn. In other words, Max Crew determines the maximum number of actions a starship can take in combat turns. This may make it seem like smaller ships are at a massive disadvantage from larger ships, and this is true. Large ships are slower, and more easily overwhelmed by swarms of smaller ships.

**Expansion Bays** represent extra space on a ship that isn't dedicated to the crew's private quarters their hygiene, or to the regular functioning of a ship. They are often prefabricated, and most starships have ports that make these bays easily detachable. Common expansion bays are the medlab, the cargo bay, or a passenger bay.

**Weapon Ports** are the number of weapons a starship can have installed on it. There are ways to increase this number, such as through modding. This number also determines a ship's number of attacks per turn.

**Mod Slots** are the number of modifications a ship can have installed. Unless otherwise stated


# Starship Combat
Starship combat is mainly focused around disabling an enemy ship, which means bringing it to 0 hit points. It is rare that ship combat results in complete destruction of a starship, as scrappers and pirates will pay handsomely for stolen starships, and those above such theft are usually seeking to bring in their targets alive.

When a starship reaches 0 hit points, it is considered disabled. It can't move, fire, or regenerate shields. The electricity switches to emergency protocol, and only basic communications capabilities and life support functions remain.

Starship rounds are best thought of as multi-minute ordeals. Starship combat is hectic. The ship flies around, crew members frantically running from station to station, all while the ship is rocked with attacks.

Flanking applies to ships

#### The Turn

**Actions:** In Starship combat, each crew member may take a starship action or a regular action, in any order they want. Ship initiative is determined by the pilot, or whoever was the last person to take a piloting action. 
**Movement:** Starships can only take two piloting actions in a turn. Movement counts as a piloting action.
**Attacking**: Starships can attack a number of times each turn equal to the number of weapons they have. The multiple attack penalty is -3/-6 and so on. The normal rules for range increments and proficiencies apply.

**Damage:** On a critical hit, double the damage dealt. If shields are down, not only does the ship take the damage, but all crew members take 1/2 damage.

#### Some Terms:
**Piloting DC:** A ship's piloting DC is determined by the last person to take a piloting action in the ship.
**Turns Its Back:** A ship turns its back when it is traveling in a straight line away from you.

The price for starships has been derived by taking the wealth per level for a party of five and doubling it, then rounding it to the nearest sane number. This assumes that a level 5 party shouldn't be too far off purchasing a medium sized starship, and that last bit of money needed to purchase it can be a great story hook!

Loans are available through the Megacorps, or the Imperial Bank. This is how most adventurers start off, although defaulting on loans can be entire adventures themselves.

When selling starships, 1/5th value is assumed when dealing with stolen or looted vessels, due to the bribes and fences required.
When selling a legally obtained starship, assume 1/2 value.

### Boarding
Often, the goal of starship combat is to gain access to the enemy ship. Why wait until it's disabled? To board an enemy ship, you must either breach the hull, or make your way through one of the locked entrances. A ship must be without a shield to land on it, but once you land on the hull, it's only a matter of using a laser cutter to cut through the exterior.

Laser Cutters are 1 bulk items about the size of a rifle. In 1 starship round, you can cut through most hulls. Once inside, it's possible to take actions on the enemy ship. You gain a bonus action, which can be used to attack the enemy 



# Starship Mods
Think of starship mods as imbued items for your ship. They are installed in a mod slot, and can be activated in or out of combat. Some need to be refreshed before they can be used again. You can own any number of mods, but your ship can only have a number installed as dictated by its size class. Changing mods is a process which takes 1 day of downtime.

### Flight Hazards
Even in the vastness of space, there are hazards that can make ship to ship combat complicated. During hyperdrive, a ship's autopilot uses the datasphere and pre-charted maps of the galaxy to maneuver around hazards on a predetermined path. During combat, there are too many variables, leaving a ship in the hands of a pilot. Nebulae, space trash, and asteroids can all present a danger to a ship's integrity.
