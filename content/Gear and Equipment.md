## Shields
Shields are roughly the size of a pocketwatch, and usually sit somewhere on the outer body. Activating a shield takes one action, and generates a field of force around a person and their armor, providing temporary hit points. This field allows gasses, but not liquids and solids, to move through it. After three rounds (roughly 20 seconds), a shield will regenerate all of its health points. Electricity deals double damage to shields. Unless otherwise stated, a shield does not inpede your movement speed or give a bonus to AC, and certain shields have effects when they are broken, or when they regenerate.

## Comlog
Anyone without a means of accessing the datasphere is woefully unequipped in a world so interconnected. Comlogs come in a variety of shapes and sizes, but are mostly implanted cybernetically or attached to the arm. They come in various styles from each of the megacorporations, but he most popular ones are from Quaasar, sporting the best cameras and sensors. All characters are assumed to own a comlog, and if one breaks, they can easily get another one for 10 units.
## Rebreather
Rebreathers recycle oxygen for the user, allowing them to breathe underwater, or in space. They are often used in industrial applications, or around dangerous gasses. Consumable filters for a rebreather are the most common, but those who use them often usually buy ones with energy field filters, which have an indefinite usage. Rebreathers fit over the face, and have a reinforced glass enclosure. They come in various shapes and sizes, but often look like a normal respirator. 
## Scanner
Scanners are handheld devices about the size and shape of a smartphone. The display shows the general surrounding area, much like a map. Activating the scanner takes three actions, which sends a series of signals out. After 10 minutes per Km, the scanner collects enough data to locate water sources, mineral deposits, and other topo-geographical features. These are usually used by surveyors and terraformers to determine the best excavation sites, but can also come in handy for cave exploration. Using a scanner gives a +5 to survival to find specific geological features, and acts as a compass when not in use.
## Holoskin
Holoskins were first used in military applications and espionage, allowing the user to shape their forms at will. With their small size and usability, they quickly found a use in civilian life. Holoskins attatch to a belt, and project an appearance of the user's choice. This acts as the spell Illusory Disguise, but you can impersonate individuals. As such, almost all businesses and public areas install devices to combat identity theft, such as signal blockers, or authentication doorways. Fantasy Planet is the largest manufacturer of Holoskins, which are used by players to imitate their characters.

## Gun Turret Kit

Turrets have the minion and tech traits, as well as any traits from the weapon mounted. Turrets can be made to attack certain targets if they have mods installed, but can always fire if movement is seen.

## Weapon Traits
**Automatic:** In addition to a normal Strike, you can fire this weapon using the Automatic Fire action.

    **Automatic Fire** :aa: (area, attack) You hit each creature in a cone with a range equal to half the weapon’s range increment. Any creatures in the area must succeed at a basic Reflex save against your class DC plus the tracking value of the weapon (you don’t roll an attack roll). This damage is area damage. Creatures that critically fail this save are subject to effects that occur on a critical hit with this weapon, including the weapon’s critical specialization effect. Automatic Fire has a usage equal to half the weapon’s maximum capacity.
    
**Area (burst, cone, line):** Weapons with this trait can only fire using the Area Fire action. 

    **Area Fire** :aa: (area, attack) You target each creature in the designated area with a range equal to the weapon’s range increment (for cone or line) or the designated radius of the explosion (for burst). For burst, you can position the center point anywhere within your first range increment. Any creatures in the area must succeed at a basic Reflex save against your class DC plus the tracking value of the weapon (you don’t roll an attack roll). This damage is area damage. Creatures that critically fail this save are subject to effects that occur on a critical hit with this weapon, including the weapon’s critical specialization effect.
    
**Concussive:** These weapons smash as much as puncture. When determining a creature’s resistance or immunity to damage from this weapon, use the weaker of the target’s resistance or immunity to piercing or to bludgeoning. For instance, if the creature were immune to piercing and had no resistance or immunity to bludgeoning damage, it would take full damage from a concussive weapon. Resistance or immunity to all physical damage, or all damage, applies as normal.

**Scatter:** This weapon fires a cluster of pellets in a wide spray. Scatter always has an area listed with it, indicating the radius of the spray. On a hit, the primary target of attacks with a scatter weapon take the listed damage, and the target and all other creatures within the listed radius around it take 1 point of splash damage per weapon damage die.


**Unwieldy:** Weapons with this trait are large and awkward, can’t be fired without cooling down first, or are otherwise difficult to use with repeated attacks. You can’t use an unwieldy weapon more than once per round and can’t use it to Strike as part of a reaction, such as Punitive Strike or Reactive Strike.

**Tech:** Weapons with the tech trait incorporate electronics, computer systems, and power sources. Sometimes the weapons use such little energy that they can rely on integrated power sources (such as melee weapons that don’t have a capacity), while others drain batteries with attacks.

**Organic**
Weapons with this trait are partly or entirely organic, often composed of a printed bone mesh, covered in bio-engineered tissues. Organic weapons generate their own "ammunition" and repair any damage done to them as long as they are well maintained, usually by feeding them and giving them access to water. Unless otherwise noted, they are not sentient. Organic weapons have twice the normal hit points of a normal item of its type. If an organic weapon ever becomes broken, it dies.

##### Stimpack
-
    The staple of modern medicine, this miracle medicine accelerates the body's natural healing. Stimpacks come in various forms, but most often come in a thin plastic tube with a spring loaded injector. They have a shelf life of roughly 3 years, even more if kept cold. Access to a stable supply of Stimpacks largely determines a faction's ability to fight. As such, all armies have scores of lab technicians specialized in creating these injectables. Though life saving in most circumstances, a Stimpack is no replacement for a medical professional, as the rapid healing process creates heavy scarring, eventually leading to much more serious issues if not treated properly. After the third stimpack of the day, all subsequent packs heal half as much (rounded up). Stimpacks and stim fluid contain nanites, and you gain the tech trait for 10 minutes after receiving a stimpack.
    Nanopack - Specialized versions of stimpacks have been made just for androids. When an android is injected with a nanopack, they recieve healing as though it were a stimpack. If a biological creature is injected however, they gain the tech trait for 8 hours.

##### Nanite Stitchings
-
	 Nanite stitching is a threat made from woven nanites. These nanites quickly get to work patching up flesh and metal alike. Nanite stitching takes 10 minutes to apply, and heals for 5d8. This counts towards a patient's use of stimpacks.

##### Bodily Stimulants
-
    The modern age is all about faster, bigger, stronger. Among the many drugs you could find on the street are Jet, Brawn, and Bulk, all of which boost the physical attributes of the body at the cost of your mental facilities. Heavy use of these drugs can not only lead to addiction, but can result in permanent damage to the personality. These drugs are all based on the same chemical backbone, and users often report a profound sense of confidence and a powerful boost in energy. 
    Bodily Stimulants are used heavily in the medical industry, where their use can be carefully controlled through IV. On the street, these drugs are taken in pill form, snorted, or inhaled through vaporizers, and they are administered in much stronger concentrations.
    **Jet:** +1 to your Dexterity Mod, but a -1 to your Wisdom Mod for 10 minutes.
    **Brawn:** +1 to your Strength Mod, but a -1 to your Intelligence Mod for 10 minutes.
    **Bulk:** +1 to your Constitution Mod, but a -1 to your Charisma Mod for 10 minutes.
    ***Addiction:*** *You take a -2 to rolls involving the depreciated attribute. Taking a dose of the drug you're addicted to will stave this off for 8 hours.*
##### Prescient
- Drug

    ***Addiction:*** *You exist in a constant brain fog, taking a -2 on all skills involving Intelligence, Charisma, and Wisdom unless you consume Prescient every two hours.*
##### Drops

Drops are highly addictive for this reason, and notoriously hard to kick. Each batch of Drops is tailored to a specific purpose, but they usually give bonuses to certain skill checks or to saving throws, with the drawback of affecting balance and becoming clumsy.
    ***Addiction l:*** *You are Clumsy 2 for as long as you are addicted to Drops. Taking a dose of Drops staves this effect off for 8 hours.*
    ***Addiction ll:*** *You are Clumsy 3 for as long as you are addicted to Drops. Taking a dose of Drops staves this effect off for 4 hours.*

##### Flashback

During these memories, you perfectly relive moments. Flashback is primarily used in cognitive behavioral therapy, and is highly regulated due to its extremely addictive nature. Regulated and patented by Rhiza, flashback was originally trialed as a truth serum of sorts, aiding investigators in criminal interrogations. After researchers discovered how clearly Flashback recalls even the faintest of memories, the company quickly rebranded the substance.

# Cybernetics

Implanting a cybernetic device requires 1 hour per 10,000c in its cost, and a DC 25 Computers and Medicine check, with failure resulting in 2 damage per 1000c in the cost.

While a person has a cybernetic upgrade, they have the tech trait.

#### Artificial eyes and Ocular Sensors
Artificial eyes are made primarily of hardened resin, coating mechanical pieces and creating a realistic prosthetic. These are mostly used by those who are born without or have lost their eyes. Ocular sensors on the other hand, are disks of metal the size of coins which replace your eyes entirely. These grant you darkvision, and provide a +2 bonus on visual perception checks. These come in all manner of shapes and colors, but the most common form is a white circle. Both Artificial eyes and Ocular Sensors have recording capabilities when paired with a Datapack or Cyberbrain.
#### Hearing Amplifiers and Echolocators
Hearing amplifiers are grafted into the inner ear, taking in auditory signals a normal ear normally couldn't. They grant a +2 on auditory perception checks. Echolocators also sit within the inner ear, but give the patient an imprecise sense of echolocation, allowing them to "see" without their eyes.
#### Datapack
Storage space never hurts, especially when it can be hidden within the base of the skull. A small chip is inserted below the ear, with wires running down the arm, allowing wireless data transfer by waving your hand over a receiver, common on many computers and servers. This chip is hard to detect, as the metals used in its construction evade metal detectors and x-rays.
#### Automated Defibrillator
Attached to the heart, this device applies electrical pulses, attempting to restore the heart to normal operation. Having an Automated Defibrillator provides a +4 to recovery checks.
#### MyVault™
Units are ephemeral, and the only way to make sure yours are safe is to keep them on your person. MyVault™ is a specialized credstick which is implanted into the forearm. Although it has wireless capabilities, these can only be used with a biosignature, and it automatically takes a biosignature from the surrounding tissue at will. If you have a cyberbrain, you can link it up with MyVault,™ barring any usage of your funds without your brain being connected to the datasphere.
#### Blood Analyzer
Inserted into the thigh, the Blood Analyzer acts as a constant biomonitor. Everything from cholesterol to microplastic levels appear on your devices, helping you plan your health. This technology is especially helpful for doctors, as having a Blood Analyzer installed gives anyone treating wounds on you a +3 on Medicine checks if they have access to the information. In addition, this device will immediately tell you if you've been poisoned or drugged.
#### Prosthetic Limb
The Ring can be a dangerous place, whether you're working in an argon refinery on Vesper, or policing the streets of Rana. Limbs are replaceable, and rather than regrow one over the course of weeks, many elect to have mechanical prosthetics. These can be made of many materials, but are usually made from a carbon fiber frame. Having a prosthetic Limb unlocks endless potential for customization of the limb, and grants an unarmed attack which deals 1d8.
#### Autoinjector
Used mostly by soldiers and the elite, an Autoinjector is a tube that is implanted into the thigh that can be loaded with 5 doses of different liquids. Using one action, you can imbibe one. Most commonly, this is a Stimpack, but it's not uncommon to have drugs or other substances injected. If you have cyberbrain, you can set up to two health thresholds, and when they are reached, you can use a reaction to take a dose.
#### Vocal Modulator
Most often used by performers, Vocal Modulators can alter your body's natural sound. This provides a +4 to Deception checks to mimic voices. If you have a cyberbrain, you can download new voices, increasing the bonus to +6.
#### Cyberbrain
The penultimate achievement in cybernetic technology, the cyberbrain is a complete human-machine interface. In this situation, the entire brain is taken and nearly half of the components are replaced with nanite-aided components. The Cyberbrain enables a constant connection to the datasphere, rendering the need for an external device moot. When connected to the datasphere, you can spend actions to download updates, giving you a bonus as per the list below. This ability can be used a number of times per day equal to your level.
Cyberbrains don't just allow software updates, but also enable secondary functions on some other cybernetics, as well as the ability to communicate nonverbally with machines and other people with cyberbrains.
A cyberbrain's most important function however, and the reason most choose to cyberize, is the ability to store ones memories and personality in a personality chip. Upon death, if a person's personality chip is intact, it can be retrieved and uploaded into a new cyberbrain. There are serious psychological consequences to this process though, as remembering one's death is traumatic.

## Personal Shields
Containment fields are commonplace in civil settings, and even more so in war. In the past few decades, the advent of personalized fields have made personal protection much easier and accessible, and are referred to as shields. Every company manufactures them, as they are the bare minimum for workplace safety. Shields, as opposed to containment fields, have no hardness, and no charge. Instead, the harness kinetic energy, recharging through the user's movement. A shield takes 1 action to toggle, and stays active until it is powered off or its shield points are reduced to 0, when it shatters in a burst of light. Shields allow gas through, but not solids or liquids, meaning they must be deactivated to eat or drink. This also means you can't be injected with poisons. A shield regenerates after 3 rounds of avoiding damage. Shields can be upgraded, and can have 2 upgrades at a time. When a shield is damaged, it shimmers a color corresponding with its energy. This shimmer can be seen idling with a DC 18 perception check.

Electric damage is doubled against shields, but is not doubled with critical strikes.

#### Power Armor
 Power armor is the final frontier of personal defense. Not only are power suits extremely resistant to damage, they add to a user's strength. While wearing power armor, you treat your strength score as though it were 4 higher. You are also immune to fall damage and penalties related to pressure, meaning you could walk on the ocean floor as easily as you could on the outside of a space station. You’re always treated as having a running start for jumps. You also gain an additional benefit depending on whether you’re wearing light or heavy power armor.

**Medium Armor**: While wearing medium power armor, you gain Resistance 2 to slashing, piercing, and bludgeoning. You can mount a one handed weapon on the pauldron of the armor, and fire it without the use of your hands.

**Heavy Armor:** While wearing heavy power armor, you gain Resistance 4 to slashing, piercing, and bludgeoning. You can mount a two-handed weapon on the pauldron of the armor, and fire it without the use of your hands.
